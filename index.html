Source: Drugs@FDA approvals report, September 2026. Covers CDER approvals (NDAs, ANDAs, CDER-reviewed BLAs); vaccines and cell and gene therapies reviewed by CBER are not included. FDA lists some approvals more than once, one row per strength or product name; "Unique approvals" counts each application and submission once (356), "Every FDA row" matches the report's 380. Therapeutic area is assigned from each drug's primary use; multi-use drugs such as rituximab or everolimus sit in their main area. Novel drugs are FDA Type 1 new molecular entities plus first-time biologics.
